# Fixture batch 137: sweeps locked to curved faces along other paths

5 cases, each profile sketched on the plane square to the path at its start, its x along the locked face's
outward normal: a 1 × 1 square and a thread's triangle along left- and right-handed helices round a cylinder
(c001, c002), a square along a helix round a cone frustum (c003), a square round the closed curve a circle beside a
cylinder splits its side along (c004), and a square round a small circle a tilted plane splits a sphere along (c005),
each locked to the face its path lies on.

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-137.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-12 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. The helix cases fail in Onshape (`SWEEP_FAILED`), as in the engine.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): a sweep locked to curved faces fails along a helix (Onshape's own fit, 7 µm off its
cylinder) but runs along an edge of the face or a circle on it; the profile turns with the face's normal along the
path. The engine carries the profile in the frame of the path's tangent and the face's normal and lofts through 192
copies: c004's ring is exact (21.040594 mm³ by Pappus, the path 21.040590 mm long), Onshape's 7.2e-4 over. Making it
showed `evDistance` giving a revolved cone's raw OCCT parameters, which `evFaceTangentPlane` read the other way round,
and the normal projection of batch 133 coming out 6 µm inside the exact curve (both fixed).
