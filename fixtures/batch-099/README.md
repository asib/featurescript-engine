Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): every direction on a sphere is principal; Onshape's least is always the normal crossed
with a world axis, n × x, n × y or n × z (normalised), and its greatest n × that. Which axis isn't the smallest of
the normal's components, nor what a symmetric eigen-solver (LAPACK's, Jacobi's) gives for n nᵀ or I − n nᵀ, nor the
parameter direction whose curvature rounds smaller; batches 109 and 110 found the rule (docs/FINDINGS.md). The directions are the same at the
same normal whatever the sphere's centre and radius (c001, c002).
