# Fixture batch 192: sketch constraints between other pairs of entities

16 cases, each a sketch drawn off and pulled into place by a constraint, returning its curves' starts, middles and

fixed fit spline (c014); a point made a fixed arc's midpoint as the UI writes it (c015); a point put 3 from the arc

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-192.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-18 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built; all match.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): two points made horizontal or vertical keep their distance, the newer turning about
the other; distances to circles and arcs are to their nearest points, on the side things start; a point goes to
the nearest point of an ellipse or a spline; a line made collinear turns about its middle and moves straight
across; a circle made coincident takes the other's centre and radius; an ellipse made horizontal turns about its
centre; `MIDPOINT` given a point and an arc (`localMidpoint`, `localEntity1`) changes nothing; a fit spline's points
are point bodies.
