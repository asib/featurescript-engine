# Fixture batch 132: conic, curvature and uneven fillets of curved edges

10 cases: a tee of a pipe of radius 10 and a branch of radius 3, its joint filleted 1 mm with conic sections (rho
0.5 and 0.7), a curvature section (magnitude 0.5) and unevenly, 1 and 2 mm, either way (c001–c005); a boss of
radius 3 rising from a lying cylinder, its foot conic (c006); a block's spherical pocket, its rim conic (c007); a
cone frustum standing on a plate, its foot conic and its top edge uneven (c008, c009); two balls united, the circle
where they meet conic (c010). Each returns its fillet's faces' areas and its edges' points at eight places round.

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-132.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-12 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): Onshape's fillets of curved edges have their feet on the rolling ball's contact curves
(within 1e-6 mm) and its sections between them as along straight edges; its faces are its own fits, 4e-5 to 7e-5
of their areas under the exact surfaces (as in batch 082). An uneven fillet's centre is `radius` from the leading
face at a convex edge, the other way round at a concave one, round a tee's joint as along straight edges; but a
cone frustum's top edge (c009) has `radius` from the top and `otherRadius` from the cone, where the leading face is
the cone: a known gap. Where each closed edge starts differs, so the edges' points are only studied.
