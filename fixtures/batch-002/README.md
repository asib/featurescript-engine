## What Onshape built

Every case built. c078 is listed as failed only because it makes nothing but a construction point and returns no
values; its probe line has the point.

Things worth knowing when matching Onshape:
- **Seam-free and vertex-free topology.** A full revolve's circular edges have no vertices (c001: 4 faces, 4 edges,
  0 vertices). A sphere is one face with no edges or vertices (c022). A scaled sphere (the ellipsoid, c024) has
  one face, no edges, and two vertices at its poles.
- **Voids.** c013 cuts a ring inside a box, leaving a void; Onshape writes it as `BREP_WITH_VOIDS`.
- **Volumes are approximate.** `evVolume` in Onshape is accurate to about 1e-7 relative on curved parts (c065's two
  halves differ by that much).
