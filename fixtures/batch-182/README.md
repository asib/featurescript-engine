# Fixture batch 182: lofts with several connections

6 cases, each probing the loft's faces with their poles, and its volume. A 20 mm square at z = 0 to a hexagon (corners
10 from the middle) at z = 20, its corners at (10, 10) and (−10, −10) connected to the hexagon's at (10, 0) and
(−10, 0) (c001); an equilateral triangle to the square, its corners at (0, 10) and (−8.66, −5) to (10, 10) and
(−10, −10) (c002); two squares, a quarter turn round by two connections (c003); circles of radius 10 at z = 0 and 20
connected from their starts and from half round the first to a third round the second (c004); a pentagon connected to
a circle three times (c005); the square connected to the circle at a corner and at its edge's middle (c006).

Files as in batch 002, with a STEP. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-182.mjs`; built in Onshape on
2026-11-16 (std 3112.0) through the UI only. c002 and c003 match; c005 and c006 on their bodies (Onshape's spans laid
out otherwise); c001 (a tie Onshape's rounding breaks) and c004 (circles connected twice, which the engine refuses)
are known gaps.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): between polygons each stretch from one connection to the next is matched as a whole
loft between polygons with different numbers of corners is, measured by the fraction of the whole perimeter.
