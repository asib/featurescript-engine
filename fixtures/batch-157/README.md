# Fixture batch 157: probes: fills with tangency conditions across a gap between sheets

4 cases, each the gap between a level sheet and one rising at 26.6° (their edges 10 apart, lines along the sides)
filled: tangent to both sheets (c001), to the level one only (c002), to the rising one only (c003), to neither
(c004). Each probe gives the fill's area, its point and normal at the middle of its parameters, and its height over
six points across the gap.

Files as in batch 002 (no STEP: the fills are sheets std leaves out of it). Generated by `node
tools/fixtures/gen-batch-157.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-13 (std 3112.0) through the UI only. None matches
yet: the engine doesn't build fills tangent to the level sheet (c001, c002), and faces the flat fill the other way
(c003, which it now builds flat as Onshape does, and c004).

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): tangent to the rising sheet only, Onshape's fill is the plane (which already continues
that sheet). Tangent to both, its heights across the gap are within 0.02 mm of the cubic with chord-length tangents
at the two edges, but vary along the gap (0.509 and 0.476 mm, 2.5 in, at 5 and 10 along) and its middle lies 3e-4 mm
off centre: its own fitted surface.
