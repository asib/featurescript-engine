# Fixture batch 121: boundary surfaces between lines with end conditions of other magnitudes and along directions

10 cases, boundary surfaces between a 30 mm line at y = 0 and one at y = 20 raised h: the first normal to its
plane at magnitudes 0.5 and 2 with h 0, 5 and 10 (c001–c006), at magnitude 1 with h 5 (c007), both normal at 2 and
1 (c008), and the first along the direction (0, 10, 5) (c009) and along (0, 10, 0), its plane's normal (c010). Each
returns its surface's poles.

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-121.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-11 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built; being sheets only, the batch has no STEP.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): between lines each curve across is the cubic leaving a conditioned line with the
magnitude times its projected chord and reaching a free one along the chord; along a direction the chord is
projected onto the plane of the direction and the line; lines in one plane make a plane.
