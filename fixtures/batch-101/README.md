# Fixture batch 101: curvature derivatives, internal tolerances, a B-spline approximation

8 cases: `evFaceCurvatureDerivative` along the tangent plane's axes on a cylinder (two parameters), a torus, a
sphere and a cone, and along a direction leaning out of the plane; `evMaxTolerance` and `evFaults` alone; a
cylinder's side as `evApproximateBSplineSurface` gives it, in full.

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-101.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-11
(std 3112.0) through the UI only. (A first build indexed the matrices with units directly, which fails in both.)

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): the derivative is of std's second fundamental form (minus each curvature times its
direction's outer product) per length along the direction, which must lie in the tangent plane (one leaning out is
refused, `NO_TANGENT_PLANE`); an extruded cylinder's parameters run as a cone's and a torus's, u from the world's
y × z round its z (back along the extrude), so its tangent plane's x points along that u; `evMaxTolerance` fails
("Execution error") while `evFaults` gives a block no faults; the cylinder's approximation starts at that x, its
half circles' poles twice the radius out.
