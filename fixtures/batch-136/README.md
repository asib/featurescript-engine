# Fixture batch 136: sketch solving: underdetermined sketches, more constraints

17 cases, each a sketch drawn off and pulled into place by constraints, returning its curves' starts, middles and
ends and its points. Underdetermined (c001–c010): a free line made 15 long; with its start fixed; with its end fixed;
with its start, then its end, coincident with a free point; a free point put on a free line; a free circle given a
radius; two free lines joined; a free line made horizontal; a free line made parallel to a fixed one. Other
constraints (c011–c017): a circle tangent to a fixed one outside it; a point on a fixed circle placed by a horizontal
distance; a point on a fixed line a distance from another; circles made equal; a line normal to a fixed circle;
points mirrored across a line; a distance between parallel lines.

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-136.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-12 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): Onshape's solver moves the newest entities and holds the older ones (a point put on a
line moves, not the line; of two lines joined the second moves, whole); it holds directions, lengths and radii over
places; a line whose length changes keeps its start, its end moving (a point coincident with the end goes with it);
a line that turns turns about its middle. The engine solves so, by projecting the start onto the constraints one
group of unknowns at a time. A circle and a line constrained to it (c015) come out with the circle's wire body first,
as the sketch made them; compared in any order.
