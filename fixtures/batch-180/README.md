# Fixture batch 180: lofts with connections between profiles other than two polygons

7 cases, each probing the loft's faces with their poles, and its volume. A 20 mm square at z = 0 to a circle of
radius 10 at z = 20: its corner at (10, 10) connected to the circle an eighth round (c001), at its start (c002), and
its corners at (10, 10) and (−10, −10) to an eighth and five eighths round (c003); the circle first, three eighths
round connected to the square's corner at (−10, 10) (c004); a hexagon, its corner at (10, 0) connected to the circle's
start (c005); the square's edge along x = 10, its middle connected to the circle's start (c006); an equilateral
triangle to the square, corner to corner (c007).

Files as in batch 002, with a STEP. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-180.mjs`; built in Onshape on
2026-11-16 (std 3112.0) through the UI only. c001, c002 and c004–c006 match loosely (their sides pole for pole,
Onshape's circular end bounded by other approximations of the arcs, 0.025 mm² and up to 0.09 mm³ off); c003 and c007
the engine doesn't build yet.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): a polygon's corners and its connection point divide the circle into as many equal arcs
from the connected point; each side is ruled between its edge and its arcs, four cubic Hermite spans for each arc;
the circle gets a vertex at every point, the connection's too.
