# Fixture batch 194: sketch constraints with ellipses, splines and curves' extensions

12 cases, each a sketch drawn off and pulled into place by a constraint, returning its curves' starts, middles and
ends and its points: a circle made tangent to a fixed ellipse (c001); a line made tangent to a fixed fit spline
(c002); a point put 2 from the ellipse (c003) and from the spline (c004); an ellipse made equal to the ellipse

fixed arc and line beyond their ends (c008, c009); a line made normal to the ellipse (c010); an ellipse mirrored
onto another across a fixed line (c011); a circle put 2 from the ellipse (c012).

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-194.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-18 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built; all match.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): distances from an ellipse or a spline are from their nearest points, a circle tangent
to an ellipse its radius from it; a line tangent to a spline slides square to itself; `EQUAL` between ellipses
changes nothing, `COINCIDENT` makes them one; a distance between lines makes them parallel, the second turning
about its middle; points on arcs and lines may lie beyond their ends; a line made normal to an ellipse turns about
where it crosses it; a mirrored ellipse keeps the original's size. A region's edges have no vertex at a closed
curve's seam where curves bounding regions meet it (c011), and a circle touching an ellipse starts both their
region edges where they touch (c001); a region sheet's edges come in the order of the sketch curves they lie on,
along each from its start (c011).
