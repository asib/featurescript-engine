# Fixture batch 156: probes: circles without radius dimensions settling against tangent lines

6 cases, circles (radii 15 and 8, 30 apart) and lines drawn a little off held tangent to them, the probe their solved
curves (sorted): two lines and two circles with only the first circle dimensioned (c001), only the second (c002),
neither and drawn second first (c003), neither with the lines drawn exactly tangent to the first (c004); one circle
between two lines (c005); two circles under one line (c006).

Files as in batch 002 (no STEP: the curves are sketch wires). Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-156.mjs`;
built in Onshape on 2026-11-13 (std 3112.0) through the UI only. Every case matches.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): with two lines and an undimensioned circle Onshape changes that circle's radius (the
second's where neither is dimensioned, whichever was drawn first) and slides the other line straight up rather than
turning it; the changed circle's centre moves along the lines' bisector, and its radius changes four fifths of what
it would with its centre held: cos²(2θ) of it for lines 2θ apart, as batch 160 found at other angles. The engine now
solves so.
