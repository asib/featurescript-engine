## What the sketch is made of (c012)

A sketch with a rectangle, two connected lines, a circle, a construction line and a point solves to:
- one wire body per sketch curve, the construction line included (8);
- one sheet body per region (2: the rectangle's and the circle's);
- a point body for the point and one for the circle's centre (2);
- no body that `qConstructionFilter(…, ConstructionObject.YES)` finds;
- `qCreatedBy(sketch, EntityType.EDGE)` finds the wire edges and the regions' edges (8 + 5), so selecting a
  sketch's curves for a surface or thin extrude needs the wire bodies' edges;
- 20 vertices: two per line's wire body, none for the circle's, four for the rectangle's region, and the two
  points.
