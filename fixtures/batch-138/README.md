# Fixture batch 138: frames locked to curved faces along other paths

5 cases, each returning its frame's parts' faces: a square tube (2 × 2, 0.2 thick), a flat bar (1 × 3) and a round
tube (radius 1, 0.2 thick) round the closed curve a circle beside a cylinder splits its side along, locked to the
side (c001–c003); a round tube round the small circle a tilted plane splits a sphere along, locked to the sphere
(c004); a square tube round the four edges an 8 × 10 rectangle beside a cylinder splits its side along, locked to the
side (c005).

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-138.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-12 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. The closed paths fail in Onshape (`FRAME_CANDIDATE_FACES`: a sweep round a closed edge
has no caps for the frame to trim), as in the engine.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): Onshape's split of a cylinder's side by a rectangle projected onto it has lines up the
side and arcs round it, four edges; the frame mitres the corners. The engine now recognizes projected edges lying
on lines and circles, keeps sharp corners when joining a split's B-spline pieces, replaces a revolved segment's end
by turning it on round its axis, and evaluates an edge OCCT split at a seam as one edge. c005 is a known gap: std
orients each segment's profile from its edge's ends and directions, which come out otherwise in the engine
(Onshape's arcs' profiles are turned 14°, the angle of the rectangle's sides round the cylinder).
