# Fixture batch 189: more curved faces replaced

5 cases, each returning the part's faces and its volume: a 20 × 20 × 10 block's top edge filleted to radius 3, the
fillet replaced up to the plane through its ends (c001); a plate's hole of radius 4 up to a coaxial cylinder of radius 3
(c002); a boss of radius 4 on a block up to a coaxial cylinder of radius 6 (c003); a square-to-circle loft's side up to
the plane x = 8 (c004); a hemispherical dome on a cylinder up to the plane z = 14 (c005).

Files as in batch 002, with a STEP. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-189.mjs`; built in Onshape on
2026-11-18 (std 3112.0) through the UI only. c001, c003 and c005 match; c002 (Onshape refuses it,
`DIRECT_EDIT_REPLACE_FACE_FAILED`) and c004 (the engine refuses it) are known gaps.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): a curved face up to a plane: the fillet becomes the chamfer (its neighbours trimmed),
the dome a flat top (the cylinder carried up to it); the loft's side a plane with its lofted neighbours and the top
extended to it; shrinking a hole is refused.
