# Fixture batch 104: a second Part Studio drawn in Onshape's UI: trimmed and used splines

A Part Studio tab ("Part Studio 3" in the fixture document) drawn by hand in Onshape's UI, its FeatureScript as its
"Show code" gives it in fixtures/blobs/ui-sketch-2.studio.fs: five sketches on Top, imported into the Feature Studio
(`Ui2::`) and built, each case one sketch's curves, vertices, regions and bodies.

| cases |
|---|
| c001: a closed spline trimmed by a line across it (`skInterpolatedSplineSegment` with `geometryIsPeriodic`), and the line |
| c002: a sketch point made by "Use" (a `PROJECTED` constraint on another sketch's vertex) |
| c003: a spline made by "Use" of the trimmed spline (`skSplineSegment`) |
| c004: a closed spline through four points |
| c005: a closed spline made by "Use" of that one (`skSpline`) |

Files as in batch 002, but no STEP: the tab's sketches make no parts. Generated by
`node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-104.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-11 (std 3112.0) through the UI only.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): a trimmed closed spline is written as a spline through its trim points and the points
left between them, with its handles' end derivatives, and is that cubic whatever `geometryIsPeriodic` says. "Use"
of a spline makes a spline by poles: its initial guess holds periodic, rational, degree, the poles' count, the
poles, the full knot vector and (open) the parameter range. A used closed spline's knots are unclamped, its degree's
multiplicity at 0 and 1 and its first pole repeated last, and Onshape flags it periodic. Neither makes point bodies.
