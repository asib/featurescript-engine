# Fixture batch 135: uneven fillets of circles; sketches solved by their constraints

20 cases. Uneven fillets, 1 and 2 mm (c001–c010): an extruded cylinder's top and bottom edges; a cone frustum's
(fCone, a revolve) top and bottom edges, standing alone, and upside down; a cylinder revolved from a rectangle; a
cylinder extruded down from its top; a hole's rim through a block; a boss's foot on a plate; a drafted extrude's top.
Each returns its part's faces' types and areas, which give each side's setback.

Sketches drawn off and pulled into place by constraints (c011–c020): a rectangle by lengths and by distances, a
square by equal sides, a circle by radius concentric with a fixed point and by diameter with its centre on one, a
line at an angle to a fixed one, a slot of tangent arcs, a point at a line's midpoint, a line mirrored across a
construction line, a 30-40-50 triangle. Each returns its sketch's edges' ends and middles.

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-135.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-12 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): an uneven fillet's `radius` is measured from the face in whose loop the edge runs
against Onshape's direction for it: an extruded cylinder's top edge from the cylinder and its bottom from the
bottom, a revolved part's edges (whose circles run back against the revolve) the other way round, a hole's rim and
a boss's foot from the plate. Onshape's uneven fillets of circles are its own fits, up to 0.13 mm³ over the exact
volumes the engine's are within 0.005 mm³ of. Onshape ignores `ANGLE` given an `angle`, and `MIDPOINT` given a
point and a line (c016, c018); it mirrors a line as a line (c019); where a sketch is underdetermined it keeps lines'
directions and lengths and moves the newest entities, and a line whose length changes keeps its start (batch 136
says more).
