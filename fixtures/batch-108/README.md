Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): tangent to a profile, a loft's side leaves each corner along the chord to its partner
projected into the plane; Onshape builds it only where that lies strictly within the corner's angle or the angle
opposite it (the turned triangles have a chord along an edge at the start and chords off both angles at the end,
whatever the magnitude; turned squares are fine). Onshape won't replace a face up to a sheet lofted between arcs (a
rational B-spline), though it will up to one lofted between lines or an extruded arc. Known gaps: the engine lofts
equal arcs into a cylinder, which it then replaces up to (c009), and can't extend the arcs' sheet (c011).
