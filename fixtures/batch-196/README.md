# Fixture batch 196: constrained surfaces through points

10 cases, each a `constrainedSurface` through points (`opPoint`), returning its face's area and its B-spline's
degrees, knots and poles: four corners of a 40 × 30 rectangle, flat (c001) and with one raised 5 mm (c002); a 3 × 3
grid over 40 × 30, flat (c003); the grid with its middle raised 4 mm, for performance (c004) and for smoothness to
0.1 mm (c005) and 0.001 mm (c006); raised 1 mm (c007); a 3 × 3 grid over 20 × 20 raised 4 mm (c008); six points not on
a grid (c009); batch 011 c006 again (c010).

Files as in batch 002. Generated by `node tools/fixtures/gen-batch-196.mjs`; built in Onshape on 2026-11-18 (std
3112.0) through the UI only. Every case built. The flat ones match (c001, c003); the others are known gaps.

Findings (docs/FINDINGS.md): every surface is a bicubic over the points' extent widened by 5 times the tolerance
each way, its parameters lengths (m) from the middle; through points in a plane it is one span, its normal the way
the first three points not in a line turn. Through other points Onshape refines the knots by a rule not found (not
monotonic in the tolerance), the same for a bump of 1 mm and of 4 mm; for performance its knots are even halvings;
for points not on a grid, or at a grid's corners raised, its parameters are its own.
